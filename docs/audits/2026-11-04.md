## Findings

Ranked by how much they matter now that 1.0 is tagged.

1. **The Daily Ride goes stale in a tab left open past 4am.** New. Small. Run.
   - The app only picks the Daily Ride when it loads (`App.tsx:90`), on import (`App.tsx:467`) and inside `finishTrip()` (`progression.ts:495`). Nothing picks again when the day turns.
   - Example: the pick on the night of 2026-11-04 is `life`. Leave the tab open and come back at 9am. The Today band and the map still show `life` as the Daily Ride (`today.ts:50-56`, `App.tsx:130`). Its intro says "Today's Daily Ride. Clear it on any tier for +100 XP." (`Intro.tsx:86`).
   - Ride it, and `finishTrip()` first picks today's line (`render`), so the ride pays no Daily Ride bonus.
   - This was already true before the 4am change (the day used to turn at midnight). It matters because Ryan plays daily, often in a tab that stays open.
   - Fix: work out the day's ride from `(save, now)` wherever it is shown, or run `ensureDailyPick()` again when the tab becomes visible.

2. **A due date in the far future is never repaired.** New, and caused by the fix. Small. Run.
   - `migrate()` keeps any finite number as `due` (`save.ts:90`). `finishTrip()` now only rewrites `due` on a first clear or a due ride (`progression.ts:538`). Before the fix, any ride overwrote a bad date.
   - A save with `due` in 2030 makes every ride "early" until 2030. That can come from a device clock that was wrong when the line was cleared, or from a hand-edited save code. In the test, 30 daily rides counted 0 reviews.
   - A `due` beyond the latest date JavaScript can store (like `1e300`) is worse. `isDue()` is false forever, `daysUntil()` and readiness come out as `NaN` (not a number), and the header's overall readiness becomes `NaN`.
   - Fix: in `finishTrip()`, treat a due date further away than its box's gap as due now. Have `migrate()` drop a `due` that is not a valid date.

3. **In zones where the clock jumps from 3am to 4am, a review can land a day late and a streak can break.** New. Small. Run.
   - Tested in `Europe/Helsinki`, `Europe/Athens` and `Europe/Kyiv`. Nine other zones came back clean, including UTC, London, Santiago, Beirut, Havana and Lord Howe.
   - The late review: `addDays()` keeps the clock time (`progression.ts:125-129`). A ride at 3:30am on 2026-03-28, plus one day, is 3:30am on the 29th. The clock skips that hour, so the browser moves it to 4:30am. That is the next app day, so the review lands a day late. The 3, 7, 14 and 30 day gaps go wrong the same way from the matching dates.
   - The broken streak: `yesterday()` (`progression.ts:138`) has the mirror problem. Ride at 8pm on 28 March, then at 3:30am on 30 March, which is still app day 29 March. The streak resets to 1 instead of growing to 2.
   - Who it affects: only rides between 3am and 4am, once a year, in about a dozen countries. It does not affect Ryan in New York.
   - Why it still matters: ADR 0027 says "a daylight saving change cannot skip or repeat a day" (`0027-early-rides-and-calendar-days.md:27`), and that is not true in these zones. The repo's year-long test (`engine.test.ts:347`) only runs in New York, where the clocks change at 2am, so it cannot catch this.
   - Fix: do the day arithmetic from noon on the app day, since no clock change ever skips noon. For example, store `due` at noon of the due day. `isDue()` and readiness only read the day, so nothing else changes.

4. **The intro says "Last done 1 days ago", and "0 days ago" after an evening rehearsal.** New. Trivial. Run.
   - `Intro.tsx:111` writes "days" whatever the number.
   - It also divides the time since the rehearsal by `DAY` and rounds it. So a rehearsal at 11pm reads "Last done 0 days ago" at 9am the next morning. This is the `t / DAY` arithmetic that `CLAUDE.md` says not to use.
   - Fix: use `-daysUntil(lastRehearsed, now)`, and say "today", "yesterday" or "N days ago".

5. **Climbing tiers in one sitting now says "This ride was early".** New. Small, and Ryan's call. Run.
   - Clear Local, then clear Express ten minutes later. The Express results screen says "This ride was early, so it did not count as a review." (`progression.ts:570`, `Results.tsx:161-166`).
   - That is true, but it happens on the main path through every new line. A ride that just unlocked Rush Hour does not feel early.
   - Fix: a softer message on a ride that raised the tier, such as "The review is still due in 1 day." without the word "early".

6. **The first end-to-end test can fail once a day at 4am.** New. Trivial. From reading the code.
   - `e2e/ride.spec.ts:246` and `:258` expect the second ride to be early. Neither the test nor the Playwright config fixes the clock or the time zone.
   - If the two rides fall either side of 4am in the browser's zone, the second ride counts as a review and the test fails. On CI, which runs in UTC, 4am is 11pm or midnight in New York.
   - Fix: Playwright's `page.clock` could pin the time. It is probably not worth more than a note in `CLAUDE.md`, next to the other end-to-end traps.

Checked and fine, so no action:

- **The map and readiness agree.** Run in eleven zones, every 15 minutes for all of 2026. On the day the map says a line is due, readiness has not dropped. After that day, it slides. There was no mismatch anywhere, Helsinki included.
- **Every gap is a whole number of days.** Run in New York, UTC, London, Santiago, Beirut, Havana, Lord Howe, Auckland, Kolkata and São Paulo. Adding 1, 3, 7, 14 or 30 days always gives exactly that many app days (`daysUntil(addDays(t, n), t) === n`), and yesterday is always exactly one day back. Finding 3 lists the exceptions.
- **The 4am boundary.** Run. A line cleared at 1am counts toward the day before. The results say "Next review in 1 day", and the line is due from 4am the same night. That is what ADR 0028 means by "a ride at 1am counts toward the day before". Its consequences already say an 11:58pm clear is due about four hours later.
- **Old saves.** From reading the code, and run for the streak case. Old due dates are still timestamps and read correctly.
  - One narrow case is not quite "harmless", as ADR 0028 puts it. The old code saved a 1am ride under the next day's date in `streak.last`. A ride at 2am the same night, under the new code, then sees `last` as a day ahead. It shows "No streak yet" and resets the streak to 1.
  - That needs the deploy to land between two rides in one night, so it is not worth fixing.
- **Badge dates and the Daily Ride's done check** both go through `dayKey()` (`progression.ts:348`, `:183`). From reading the code.
- **An early ride always shows a real "still due in N days" number.** A ride only counts as early when `daysUntil()` is above 0, so N is at least 1. From reading the code.

## Interview questions

1. **When is a review due?** `isDue()` at `progression.ts:141-142`, [ADR 0027](../adr/0027-early-rides-and-calendar-days.md). A line is due from the start of its due day, not from the exact minute it was last ridden, so riding at 7pm counts even if you cleared it at 8pm. The check counts whole app days between now and the due date with `daysUntil()`, and anything at 0 or below is due.

2. **What happens if you ride a line before it is due?** `progression.ts:529-538` and `:570`. It is extra practice: you still get XP, tier progress and the streak, but the line's review step (its Leitner box) and its due date stay where they were, and the results screen says the ride was early. Before the fix, an early ride pushed the due date back, so riding a line every day meant its review never came.

3. **Why does the app's day start at 4am?** `DAY_STARTS` at `progression.ts:62`, [ADR 0028](../adr/0028-the-day-starts-at-4am.md). With a midnight boundary, a line cleared at 11:58pm was due two minutes later, and a 1am session counted as a new day. Starting the day at 4am, as the flash-card app Anki does, moves that edge to an hour when people are asleep. One function, `dayKey()`, then decides what "today" is for reviews, the streak, the Daily Ride and badges.

4. **How do you handle daylight saving?** `addDays()` and `daysUntil()` at `progression.ts:125-135`. A new due date is "n calendar days later at the same clock time" rather than "plus n times 24 hours", and day counts compare both dates at noon and round, so a 23 or 25 hour day still counts as one. The tests run in New York time (`vite.config.ts`), because UTC has no clock changes to catch. The honest follow-up is finding 3: in zones that jump from 3am to 4am, a ride in that hour can still land a day late.

5. **How does readiness relate to the "Review" flag on the map?** `lineReadiness()` at `progression.ts:209-230`. Readiness is a 0 to 1 score per line, built from the tier cleared, the review step and how many questions you answer cleanly. It starts to slide only after the due day ends at 4am the next morning, then drops a little each hour, down to half. So on the day the map says "Review", readiness has not dropped yet, and the two never disagree.

6. **How do you test code that depends on the clock?** `engine.test.ts:280` and `:347`. Every scheduling function takes `now` as a parameter that defaults to `Date.now()`, so a test can pass in any moment it wants, like ten days of 7pm rides after an 8pm clear. Every test run uses New York time, so a test means the same on every machine. A bug planted on purpose (plain 24-hour days) was confirmed to fail the tests.

7. **Did the fix need a save migration?** `save.ts:90`, [ADR 0015](../adr/0015-repair-damaged-saves.md). No: `due` is still a timestamp, and only the way it is read changed, so old saves just work. A new field would have needed a default in `emptySave()` and a line in `migrate()`, because `migrate()` rebuilds the save field by field and drops anything it does not list.
